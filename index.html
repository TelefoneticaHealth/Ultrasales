FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Telefonetica Health HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Calls, video visits, and messages are encrypted, and we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every customer.

Can we keep our existing phone numbers?

Yes. We port your current numbers for you, and patients keep calling the same numbers with no downtime.

How long does setup take?

Most single-location practices are live within a few days. Multi-location rollouts are scheduled to fit your operations.

Do patients need to download an app for video visits?

No. Patients join from a secure link in their browser or text message.

Does it integrate with our other software?

CRM integration is included on Pro Plus. Tell us what you use during your demo and we'll walk you through the options.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Service is month-to-month, and new accounts get 14 days free.

Do we have to buy desk phones?

No. Your team can make and take calls from the Ooma mobile and desktop apps. Desk phones are optional.
