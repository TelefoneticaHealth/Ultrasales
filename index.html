The problem

Your phones are costing you patients and revenue.

Most practices run on a patchwork of old phone systems, a separate video tool, and sticky notes. Patients notice.

  • Calls go unanswered at peak hours, and those patients book somewhere else.
  • Staff spend hours on reminder calls, callbacks, and phone tag.
  • Video visits run on consumer apps that weren't built for healthcare.
  • No-shows leave empty slots on the schedule every week.
  • You can't see call volume, wait times, or missed opportunities.

What a missed call really costs

Multiply the calls you miss each day by your new-patient conversion rate and the value of a patient over a lifetime. For most clinics the total is far more than the cost of fixing the phones.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Telefonetica Health HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Calls, video visits, and messages are encrypted, and we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every customer.

Can we keep our existing phone numbers?

Yes. We port your current numbers for you, and patients keep calling the same numbers with no downtime.

How long does setup take?

Most single-location practices are live within a few days. Multi-location rollouts are scheduled to fit your operations.

Do patients need to download an app for video visits?

No. Patients join from a secure link in their browser or text message.

Does it integrate with our EHR or scheduling system?

Integrations are available on Enterprise plans. Tell us what you use during your demo and we'll walk you through the options.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month, and annual plans are available at a discount.
